🔥 The AI Waste Problem

You gave your staff a Ferrari.
They're using it to check cricket scores.

When companies roll out AI tools without governance, the waste is immediate, invisible, and expensive. Here's what we see every single day.

🌤️
Off-Topic Queries

Weather, recipes, personal errands

Staff treating company-paid GPT-4o as a personal assistant. Weather forecasts, travel planning, birthday wish generators, astrology readings — all on your token budget.

Avg 23% of all queries are off-topic in ungoverned orgs
🔁
Duplicate Work

Same prompt. 14 departments. 14 bills.

Marketing, Sales, and Engineering independently ask AI to summarise the same competitor report, generate the same market analysis, or write the same onboarding doc — every week.

Teams run duplicate queries 6.4× per month on average
🐘
Prompt Hoarding

Massive context. Minimal return.

Developers pasting entire codebases into prompts for a one-line fix. Analysts uploading 500-page PDFs to ask a single yes/no question. You're paying for 200,000 tokens for a 3-word answer.

Avg 34% of token spend is oversized context
👤
Shadow AI

Personal accounts, company data

Employees use personal ChatGPT or Claude accounts for work tasks — meaning your customer data, financials, and source code leave the building without any trace or control.

67% of enterprise employees use personal AI accounts for work
🔓
Data Leakage

PII, source code, trade secrets

Customer Aadhaar numbers in support prompts. Salary data in HR queries. Unreleased product roadmaps in marketing briefs. All flowing to public LLMs with no interception.

1 in 8 enterprise prompts contains sensitive data
💀
Zombie Subscriptions

Paying for tools nobody uses

Teams sign up for GPT-4, Claude Pro, Gemini Advanced, Perplexity, and Midjourney — then use only one. You're paying for 5 seats × 50 staff across tools nobody opened last month.

Avg enterprise wastes $1,800/month on unused AI subscriptions
🎯
Wrong Model Selection

Using GPT-4o to spell-check an email

Your team defaults to the most expensive model for every task. A spell-check doesn't need GPT-4o. A quick summary doesn't need Claude Opus. Smart routing fixes this automatically.

Up to 60% of queries could run on cheaper models
📉
Zero ROI Visibility

You spend ₹10L/month. On what, exactly?

The CFO asks what the AI budget delivered this quarter. Nobody knows. No breakdown by team, no output quality scores, no way to justify renewal — or cut waste.

92% of CTOs can't explain their AI ROI to the board

Token Maxxing

Stuffing prompts to squeeze every token

Developers have discovered that padding prompts with filler text, repeating instructions, or injecting fake "system context" tricks LLMs into longer, costlier responses — intentionally burning your budget. Some do it to look productive. Others just don't know better.

Detected in 1 in 12 enterprise orgs — avg ₹22,000 extra spend/month
